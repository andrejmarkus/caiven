# Caiven creator rights

This policy covers the games and carts you make with Caiven.

## You own your games

You keep ownership of the code, art, music, sound effects, writing and
other content you create for a Caiven cart. Using Caiven doesn't transfer
any of it to the Caiven project or its maintainers.

## You can sell them

You can publish, sell, license or otherwise make money from games made with
Caiven. You don't need to:

- pay the Caiven project royalties or a revenue share;
- buy a commercial-use license;
- ask the maintainers for permission; or
- publish your game's source code.

You are responsible for checking that any third-party code, assets, fonts,
music or other material in your game allows the use you have in mind.

## Carts and exported games

A `.cav` cart you create is your content. The project doesn't treat it as a
derivative work of Caiven only because it runs on the Caiven runtime or
calls Caiven's public APIs.

If you export a game and it ships with Caiven runtime code, that runtime
code stays under its own license. Your own code and assets can use whatever
terms you choose, within the licenses of any third-party material you
include.

## Paid services

The project may charge for optional things like official hosting, cloud
features, support, asset packs or signed builds. Paying or not paying for
them doesn't change who owns your games.

## No lock-in

The project intends to keep the local editor, the runtime, loading and
saving carts, and normal game distribution working without a required
online account or revenue share.
